# 🌾 KethPiyasa (කෙත්පියස)
> **Direct Farmer-to-Buyer B2B Agricultural Marketplace Platform**

KethPiyasa is a full-stack B2B digital marketplace engineered to streamline Sri Lanka's agricultural supply chain. By bridging smallholder farmers directly with commercial buyers (supermarkets, food processors, exporters, and wholesale distributors), KethPiyasa eliminates intermediary exploitation, minimizes post-harvest losses, and ensures transparent, escrow-secured trading.

---

## 📌 Key Features

* **🌾 Farmer Produce & Inventory Management:** List current stock and upcoming pre-harvest yields with standard grading (Grade A, B, C), transparent bulk pricing, and harvest schedules.
* **🔍 Buyer Discovery Engine:** Advanced filtering by crop variety, quality grade, harvest timeline, and geographic proximity.
* **🤝 Quotation & Negotiation System:** Direct real-time bidding, counter-offer mechanisms, and forward contracting.
* **🔒 Escrow Payment Gateway:** Safe transactional holding system releasing payouts to farmers only upon verified produce delivery and inspection.
* **🚚 Logistics & Fleet Coordination:** Third-party hauler dispatch workflows with real-time tracking and QR/digital signature verification.
* **🛡️ Admin Governance & Market Indicators:** Role-based verification, dispute resolution handling, and live national economic center baseline price tracking.
* **🌐 Trilingual Localization:** Fully localized UI in Sinhala (සිංහල), Tamil (தமிழ்), and English.
